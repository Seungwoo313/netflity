Open this page in Firefox on your tablet. Netflity runs in Firefox for Android. Get Firefox

  1. Add Netflity to Firefox

    Netflity is installed.

    Add to Firefox
    It only downloaded a file?
    1. In Firefox, open Settings → About Firefox and tap the Firefox logo 5 times.
    2. Go back to Settings and tap Install extension from file.
    3. Pick netflity.xpi from Downloads, then tap Add.

  2. Open Netflix

    Open Netflix

    Sign in if Netflix asks. If Firefox offers to open the Netflix app, stay in Firefox.

  3. Join the room

    Netflity opens on the right side of Netflix with the room code already filled in. Type your name and tap Join.
